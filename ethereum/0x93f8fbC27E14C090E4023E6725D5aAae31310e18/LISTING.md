The contract source must be verified before an explorer will take a logo submission.

Etherscan shows a logo after the owner submits the update described at https://info.etherscan.com/how-to-update-token-info/ and Etherscan approves it.

Blockscout shows a logo after the owner verifies ownership and Blockscout approves it (https://docs.blockscout.com/using-blockscout/overviews/token-info), or after CoinGecko lists the token.

Trust Wallet's asset repository does not accept brand-new tokens (https://github.com/trustwallet/assets). logo.png and info.json are the pack to submit once the token qualifies.

MetaMask auto-detect reads upstream lists (https://support.metamask.io/develop/how-to-add-a-token-logo). The add page is the path that can show the logo on deploy day.
